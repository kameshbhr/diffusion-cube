Low-resource language AI adoption: playbook

For a district or state champion bringing a tribal or low-resource language onto India’s language AI infrastructure. Built on the Pathway Model: where it applies, what it enables, the jobs to do, insights across them, and how proven it is.

Solid tile: where the job starts Dashed tile: the job continues in this stage ↻ the job recurs across build rounds Who owns: the roles that own the job’s checklist items, with PMU in bold. Who tracks: PMU tracks every job. “PMU owns” shows only the jobs where PMU is an owner. The Nodal Officer is a separate role from PMU.

Stage, work area and job type placements are a first mapping of the playbook’s content onto the jobs framework. Where the content gives no timing, the placement is an assumption.
